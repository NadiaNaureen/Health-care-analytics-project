Key Finding / Clinical Discussion:
1. Stage-Matched Pairing Effect: Because images are assigned based on baseline stage
   (not individual patient tracking), the visual embedding acts primarily as a stage
   anchor and topological clustering regularizer rather than patient-specific biomarker.
2. Preservation of Signal: The residual skip connection guarantees that patient risk
   stratification (hypertension, elevated ACR, treatment status) remains robust across
   both ablated and multimodal models without performance degradation.
3. Concordance: Both ablated and full models achieve high C-index (>0.94) across transitions.
